Ranking (score out of 5)

Why premixes over RTC mixes, though RTC scores higher. RTC is bigger (₹9,800 Cr) and faster growing (17%), so it wins on size and growth. Premixes wins every Board-fit test: brand adjacency 4.6 vs 3.6, low capex, 42-50% gross margin, and it peaks Oct-Feb when our plant runs at 34%. Choosing it is a deliberate call on Tension 03 (adjacency over attractiveness). Namkeen is large and counter-seasonal but fails the budget constraint: it needs a plant.
